About me

I’m Rejan Isufi, a student who cares about doing thoughtful work and growing skills that will matter after graduation.

Why I made this site

I built this site for a class assignment to practice structuring content in plain HTML and styling it with CSS—no frameworks—so I really understand how pages are put together. It is also a small portfolio piece: a place where a professor or future collaborator can see how I present myself in writing and in code.

What I’m aiming for

My goals are to keep improving at clear communication, at organizing information so it is easy to scan, and at writing markup that is accessible and semantic. Longer term, I want to combine those strengths with whatever technical path I choose next—whether that is more front-end work, data, or another field where attention to detail matters.

What I’m learning right now

Through this project I am learning how layout, typography, and color choices affect readability, how to link related pages into a simple site, and how to explain my own story in a way that feels honest and specific instead of generic. I am also getting comfortable validating my HTML and iterating based on feedback—exactly the kind of process I expect to use on future team projects.
